The Void - sound effects

All sound effects are by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Packs:
  Sci-Fi Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  Digital Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio

Each file was trimmed, peak-normalised, converted to mono 32 kHz .ogg (Vorbis q1) and .mp3 (48 kbps);
loops (jet, thrust, laser) were made seamless with a 0.3 s crossfade. sfx-data.js is a base64 copy of the .ogg files for file:// play.
